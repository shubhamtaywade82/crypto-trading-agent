## 2. Context discovery — exploratory experiment

Experiment `EXP-CONTEXT-2026-11-10-0624Z-001` used 499 completed 4h candles per symbol (2026-08-19 through 2026-11-10 03:59:59 UTC). After 50-bar warmup, 450 observations were split chronologically 315/67/68.

Features:
- trend strength = (EMA20 − EMA50) / Wilder ATR14;
- volatility = ATR14 / close;
- directional trend threshold = training q67 of absolute trend strength;
- volatility buckets = training q33/q67 of ATR14/close;
- thresholds frozen before validation/test classification.

| Symbol | Train q67 abs strength | ATR% q33/q67 | Validation→test occupancy TVD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BTC | 0.901 | 0.975 / 1.129 | 0.866 |

The validation-to-test occupancy shift is substantial for all four symbols. SOL and XRP validation windows were dominated by TRANSITION_LOW (79.1% and 80.6%), while their test windows shifted toward BEAR_LOW / TRANSITION_HIGH. This is evidence of distribution drift in this short sample, not proof that the classifier itself is defective.
