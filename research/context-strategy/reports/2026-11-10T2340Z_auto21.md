## 2. Context discovery — EXP-CTX-4H-001

**Question:** does a compact trend × volatility taxonomy fitted on training data retain useful distribution/transition structure on later chronological segments?

**Inputs and method**

- Four 4h symbols; 13,454 aligned bars each; 53,816 total.
- Raw page files: nine per symbol; feature extraction checked duplicates, 4h continuity, monotonicity, OHLC invariants, positive volume and finite values. All checks were zero; timestamps aligned across symbols.
- Feature warmup: 100 bars. Train/validation/test indices: [0,8072), [8072,10763), [10763,13454). Effective rows per symbol: 7,972 train, 2,691 validation, 2,691 test.
- Features: EMA20–EMA50 spread as % of close; 20-bar close return; TR14 simple moving average as % of close.
- Pooled thresholds fitted only on train: absolute EMA spread Q33 = **1.0301%**; TR14-SMA volatility Q33 = **1.9012%**, Q67 = **3.0433%**, Q99 = **11.0656%**.
- Labels: UP/DOWN/SIDEWAYS/TRANSITION × LOW/NORMAL/HIGH, with SHOCK overriding when volatility exceeds the train Q99. No strategy or future-return label was used.

**Combined context frequency**

| Context | Train | Validation | Untouched test |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SIDEWAYS_LOW | 18.90% | 24.12% | **36.87%** |
| UP_HIGH | 11.02% | 4.87% | 0.94% |
| UP_NORMAL | 10.43% | 11.24% | 5.76% |
| DOWN_HIGH | 10.04% | 6.73% | 4.31% |
| DOWN_LOW | 4.72% | 6.33% | **12.35%** |
| SIDEWAYS_HIGH | 4.33% | 1.83% | 0.73% |
| TRANSITION_HIGH | 6.61% | 2.80% | 0.59% |
| SHOCK | 1.00% | 0.00% | 0.00% |

**Duration and transitions**

- SIDEWAYS_LOW mean duration: 17.37 bars / 69.5h train; 16.23 bars / 64.9h validation; 19.17 bars / 76.7h test.
- TRANSITION contexts are shorter-lived, generally around 2.8–4.1 bars (11–16.5h).
- The dominant transitions are self-transitions, especially SIDEWAYS_LOW → SIDEWAYS_LOW; the taxonomy mostly identifies persistent states rather than one-bar noise.
- Test SIDEWAYS_LOW share varies materially by symbol: BTC 55.0%, ETH 34.5%, SOL 25.0%, XRP 32.9%. A pooled label does not imply identical symbol-level context distributions.

**Predeclared stability gates**

| Metric | Result | Gate |
|---|---:|---|
| Train–validation frequency Pearson | 0.855 | ≥0.70 — pass |
| Train–test frequency Pearson | 0.718 | ≥0.70 — pass |
| Train–validation Jensen–Shannon divergence | 0.0349 | ≤0.15 — pass |
| Train–test Jensen–Shannon divergence | 0.1260 | ≤0.15 — pass |
| Test contexts below 1% | 4 | zero sparse contexts — fail |

**Conclusion: taxonomy stability gate FAILED.** UP_HIGH (0.94%), SIDEWAYS_HIGH (0.73%), TRANSITION_HIGH (0.59%) and SHOCK (0%) are too sparse in the untouched test. The test also shifts toward SIDEWAYS_LOW and DOWN_LOW while high-volatility states decline. Do not retune against this test. Keep this taxonomy exploratory; a simpler volatility partition can be preregistered using training/validation only and evaluated on a genuinely new future holdout.

This experiment measures context prevalence and persistence only. It does not estimate trade win probability, target-before-stop probability, expectancy or strategy OOS.

## 4. Key discoveries

- The 4h context taxonomy is useful descriptively but fails the predeclared sparse-context gate. High-volatility and shock buckets are not stable enough to use as hard strategy filters.
- SIDEWAYS_LOW prevalence rose from 18.9% train to 36.9% test; DOWN_LOW rose to 12.35%. This is a context-distribution shift, not evidence of a profitable mean-reversion edge.
- The tested SOL SIDEWAYS-LOW failed-breakout fade is rejected for its tested rules. This does not establish that all range-fade variants fail.
- Neither Donchian20 version is actionable: inherited-v75 has only 12 trades and missing provenance; V1 has 55 trades, a CI crossing zero and negative quote PnL.
- No validated edge exists for SOL, ETH or XRP. No evidence supports a 1–10% move-capture probability or a profitable scale-out ladder.
