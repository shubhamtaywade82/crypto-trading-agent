## 2. Context discovery

- Trend strength: `(EMA20 - EMA50) / Wilder ATR14`.
- Trend state: TRANSITION when absolute trend strength is below the training q67; otherwise BULL or BEAR by sign.
- Volatility state: LOW below training ATR/close q33, NORMAL between q33 and q67, HIGH at/above q67.
